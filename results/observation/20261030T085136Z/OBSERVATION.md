## 1. Re-detection and the technical noise floor (DESIGN §3.3 (i))
- **Sample.** 2,000 tier-1 proteins with seed 0 (prereg meta.seeds[0]). Eligible proteins are single-fragment F1
  models with status ok, ≥ 1 kept atlas pocket and a Foldcomp v4 entry. The sample is stratified by kingdom
  (Eukaryota / Bacteria / Archaea) × structure pLDDT band, with equal allocation per cell. Archaea have only 39 proteins
  below pLDDT 70, so the remainder was spread over the other cells. The seeds subset is 300 proteins, stratified the
  same way.
- **Sequence check.** The v4 sequence differs from the v6 sequence for 8 of the 2,000 proteins (7 differ in
  length). These 8 were skipped and counted.
- **Method record.** The atlas files and every comparison table share method-record hash b110dd…19b1. That hash
  covers the fingerprint-defining fields of the `pocketcompare` schema metadata; backend, device and storage are
  excluded. `method_hash_ok = true`.
  - The seed runs use `scripts/observation/describe_burrow_seed.py`. It is the frozen method with only the BURROW
    model file swapped. The swap is recorded in the schema key `pocketevo_burrow_model`; the method record cannot
    show it.
  - The s0 control reproduces the atlas exactly: re-detection 1.0, cosine 1.0. This confirms the driver.
- **Matching rule.** Sites are built with `pocketevo.sites.build_sites` on both sides. A v6 atlas site counts as
  re-detected if the best site of the same protein in the other source has residue Jaccard ≥ τ. τ = 0.3 is
  provisional, and the full grid {0.2, 0.3, 0.4, 0.5} is in `redetection.json` / `redetection_strata.csv`. Cosine is
  computed on the representative fingerprints of matched pairs.

Results at τ = 0.3 (see OBSERVATION_numbers.md for every stratum):

**Model source: Foldcomp v4 against the AFDB v6 file.** These are different AF predictions plus Foldcomp
compression.
- Re-detection is 0.653 with the atlas filters applied to v4 and 0.676 with no filters.
- By band: 0.574 at footprint pLDDT 70–90 and 0.716 at ≥ 90.
- By burrow score: 0.57 below 0.1, 0.74 at 0.1–0.3 and 0.81 at ≥ 0.3.
- By kingdom it is flat (0.64–0.66).
- Matched-pair cosine: median 0.86, p10 0.64, p05 0.53.

**BURROW training seed, s1 / s2 on the v6 model.**
- Re-detection is 0.807 for s1 and 0.818 for s2; it is 0.76–0.78 for score < 0.1 and 0.87–0.89 for score ≥ 0.3.
- Matched-pair cosine: median 0.99, p10 0.80–0.81, p05 0.66–0.71.

**No footprint band below 70 exists**, because the atlas keeps only pockets with lining pLDDT ≥ 70. The structure
pLDDT bands are in `redetection_sites.parquet` (`protein_band`).

**Implications.**
- The same-protein re-detection ceiling for M1 is about 0.65 across sources and about 0.81 across seeds. Cross-source
  comparisons (v4 tier 3 / tier L against v6 tiers 1–2) therefore lose about a third of sites on technical grounds
  alone, even within one protein.
- The noise floor for specificity shifts (prereg noise_floor_quantile 0.95) is a cosine of about 0.53 across sources
  and about 0.66–0.71 across seeds, taken as the 5th percentile of same-site cosines. Within one source the floor is
  much tighter: median 0.99.
- Case studies should re-describe all members from one source (DESIGN §1). Tier L is built for exactly that: it uses
  the same source as tier 3.

## 2. False-A on known-present sites and rescue (DESIGN §3.3 (ii), §12 item 7)
**Known sites.** 73,107 experimentally evidenced sites in 16,999 tier-1/2 proteins (single-fragment F1 models):
- **BioLiP2**: 58,457 sites.
  - Only the `regular` and `metal` classes are used; peptide, DNA and RNA are excluded.
  - At least 80 % of the site's residues must be mapped to UniProt.
  - Duplicates are collapsed per protein × ligand class: largest first, keeping a site only if its Jaccard with
    every kept site is below 0.5. This turns 382,679 per-PDB rows into 58,457 sites.
- **UniProt binding sites** with ECO:0000269 / ECO:0007744 evidence: 8,330, grouped per ligand.
- **LIGYSIS af_set** (shipped with burrow): 6,320.

**Ligand class.** metal / cofactor / small molecule. For BioLiP2 this uses the CCD class plus a cofactor CCD list;
for UniProt, ion-name and cofactor keywords; for LIGYSIS, ion_prop = 1 means metal. The lists are in
`false_a.py`.

**Sequence check.** BioLiP2 and UniProt sites are skipped when the v6 model sequence differs from the current
UniProt sequence: 106 skipped, all counted.

**A criteria (the state caller is blind to labels).**
- Region pLDDT of the known residues in the atlas v6 model: mean ≥ 90 and a fraction below 70 of ≤ 0.2. The
  sensitivity stratum is mean ≥ 70.
- No overlap with UniProt SIGNAL or TRANSMEM features (prereg overlap max 0).
- The same-protein design makes the aligned-fraction, length and truncation criteria trivially satisfied.

**Detection rule.** A known site counts as detected if an atlas site covers ≥ 50 % of its residues.

**False-A rate (primary): 0.399** (21,127 of 52,949 A-eligible sites). The tune and test halves give 0.407 and 0.392.
- **By ligand class:** cofactor 0.225, small molecule 0.356, metal 0.609.
- **By site size:** 0.534 for 3–5 residues, 0.313 for 6–10, 0.200 for 11–20 and 0.360 for > 20.
- **By source:** BioLiP2 0.387, UniProt 0.315, LIGYSIS 0.656.
- **By tier:** tier 1 0.421, tier 2 0.365.
- **By organism:** for organisms with ≥ 300 A-eligible sites the range is 0.31–0.58. Examples: human 0.465, E. coli
  0.344, mouse 0.426, yeast 0.450, M. tuberculosis 0.331, Arabidopsis 0.338, T. thermophilus 0.580. All organisms
  are in `false_a_strata.csv`.
- **By coverage threshold:** 0.245 if any overlap counts as detected, 0.312 at ≥ 30 % and 0.567 at ≥ 70 %.
- The sensitivity stratum (pLDDT ≥ 70) gives 0.415.
- A first LIGYSIS-only run is kept in `ligysis_only_v1/`.
- **Reading:** high-confidence models do *not* make absence calls safe. The misses are mostly small sites, metal
  sites and sites of 5 residues or fewer.

**Rescue settings.** `pocketevo.rescue` runs six settings, each one change from the atlas-like base
(`burrow predict --af-mode --output-rule region`):
- `--min-score none` (without region, which burrow refuses in combination);
- no AF mode;
- model seed s1;
- model seed s2;
- preset fast;
- preset accurate.

A setting hits a footprint if one of its pockets has residue Jaccard ≥ 0.3 with it. Coverage ≥ 0.5 is reported as a
sensitivity rule in `rescue_k_table.csv`. Burrow outputs for 2,612 models are cached under
/tmp/pe_big/obs/falseA/rescue.

**What the rescue was run on.**
- 2,500 false-A cases, a seeded sample stratified by source × half × band × ligand class.
- 2,454 true-negative controls. Each is a random surface patch of the same size in the same protein: residues with
  ≤ 18 CB neighbours within 10 Å, the seed residue plus its nearest surface residues spanning ≤ 16 Å. The patch must
  pass the same A pLDDT criteria and overlap no atlas site and no known site. No such patch could be found for 46
  cases.
- False-A after rescue at k is estimated as false-A × P(< k settings hit | false-A case).

| k | false-A after rescue, TUNE | TEST | controls rescued, TUNE (true negatives turned into W) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0.129 | 0.142 | 0.348 |
| 2 | 0.201 | 0.206 | 0.113 |
| 3 | 0.237 | 0.235 | 0.062 |
| 4 | 0.256 | 0.259 | 0.038 |

**Choice of k (prereg rule, applied literally as the PI instructed): no k in {1..4} meets the 5 % target on the
TUNE half.**
- `rescue_k.json` therefore records `k = null` and `target_met = false`, with `k_min_false_A = 1`.
- If the PI adopts k = 1, the most conservative choice toward absence claims:
  - residual false-A is about 0.13–0.14;
  - about 35 % of true-negative regions are rescued to W, so roughly 65 % of real absences survive as A.

**Breakdown by site size and ligand class** (all halves pooled; `rescue_breakdown.csv` also has the tune / test
split and pLDDT-band and kingdom strata):

| stratum | false-A before | after, k = 1 | after, k = 2 | controls rescued, k = 1 | controls rescued, k = 2 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| all | 0.399 | 0.136 | 0.204 | 0.350 | 0.119 |
| ≤ 10 residues | 0.447 | 0.158 | 0.237 | 0.358 | 0.123 |
| > 10 residues | 0.225 | 0.054 | 0.082 | 0.270 | 0.076 |
| cofactor | 0.225 | 0.079 | 0.133 | 0.329 | 0.100 |
| metal | 0.609 | 0.132 | 0.197 | 0.337 | 0.111 |
| small molecule | 0.356 | 0.159 | 0.233 | 0.367 | 0.131 |

**Suggestion for the PI (not applied; it would be a dated deviation):** restrict A calls to footprints with more
than 10 residues. Rescue at k = 1 then gives false-A 0.054 (0.054 on the tune half too), roughly the 5 % target, at
a cost of 27 % of control regions rescued.

**Table for the simulation agent: `error_rates.parquet`.** It is a long table. Columns:
- `metric`: redetect / false_A / false_A_after_rescue / control_rescued
- `criterion`, `model_source` (afdb_v6, foldcomp_v4_kept, foldcomp_v4_all, seed_s0/s1/s2), `kingdom`, `taxid`
  (string), `plddt_band`, `site_size_class` (≤ 10 / > 10), `ligand_class`
- `tau` (re-detection only, grid 0.2–0.5), `k` (rescue rows), `n`, `n_events`, `rate`, `ci_lo`, `ci_hi` (Wilson 95 %)

The value `all` marks a pooled margin. Rows with `kingdom = by_taxid` carry per-species rates.

## Caveats
- τ = 0.3 is provisional. Re-run `analyze_redetection.py` and `error_rates.py` with the final τ (≈ 30 s).
- The rescue hit rule (Jaccard ≥ 0.3) is the P-call rule. It should be re-run if τ changes: the burrow outputs are
  cached, so this takes minutes.
- Control patches are "non-site" only with respect to known and atlas sites. Some may be real undetected sites,
  which biases the control rescue rate upward.
- The false-A estimate after rescue rests on a sample of 2,003 of the A-primary cases.
- Interface and oligomer rules were not applied. Metal sites dominate the residual false-A.
