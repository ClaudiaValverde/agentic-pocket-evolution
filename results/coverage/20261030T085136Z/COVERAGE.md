## 6. Biases & caveats

- **Superkingdom composition of done tier-3 entries vs all tier 3:** Bacteria: 50.5% of done vs 70.4% of all tier-3 entries (×0.72); Eukaryota: 13.6% of done vs 25.8% of all tier-3 entries (×0.53); Archaea: 25.4% of done vs 2.7% of all tier-3 entries (×9.31); Viruses: 0.0% of done vs 0.0% of all tier-3 entries (×9.31); unclassified: 10.5% of done vs 1.1% of all tier-3 entries (×9.31).
- **Whole superkingdoms already exhausted:** Archaea (5,826,915/5,826,915 entries done), unclassified (2,417,979/2,417,979 entries done), Viruses (41/41 entries done). The round-robin interleaves superkingdoms, so small superkingdoms finish first and later units are almost all Bacteria/Eukaryota: the composition of the done subset will keep shifting until the run ends; any done/pending comparison is a snapshot property, not a stable one. Remaining fraction done: Bacteria 7.7%, Eukaryota 5.7%.
- **Round-robin over the tree flattens clade sizes:** the done subset is deliberately broad, not proportional. Over-represented phyla (≥200k entries): (none) ×9.3, Thermoproteota ×9.3, Nitrososphaerota ×9.3, Candidatus Rokuibacteriota ×9.3, Ignavibacteriota ×9.3; under-represented: Pseudomonadota ×0.05, Streptophyta ×0.05, Actinomycetota ×0.12, Bacillota ×0.14, Rotifera ×0.14. Small/rare clades are over-sampled relative to the giant bacterial phyla. Any statistic pooled over tier 3 must be weighted (per taxon / per clade) or compared against the matching pending set.
- **Unit composition:** done units average 2,929 structures and 2.3 taxa/unit vs 2,633 and 6.2 for pending units.
- **Protein size:** median Foldcomp entry size done 4007 B vs pending 4441 B (≈ 0.062 residues/byte, r = 1.00).
- **Partial proteomes:** of tier-3 taxa with ≥1 done entry, most are represented by a slice; only 3,703 taxa with ≥1,000 entries are ≥90 % present. Presence/absence (gain/loss) calls across tier-3 species are not interpretable until the species' full entry set is done — restrict such analyses to tier 1 (complete reference proteomes) + tier-3 taxa flagged complete, or condition on `frac_present`.
- **Slices are contiguous, not random:** within a taxon, units are consecutive runs of Foldcomp-DB entries (DB offset order, which follows accession/key order and hence often one UniProt proteome submission). A done slice of a species is therefore not a random sample of its proteome.
- **Unclassified / metagenomic:** in done tier 3, 10.3% of entries are from metagenome taxa (vs 1.1% of all tier 3), 0.3% are other non-metagenome unclassified taxa, 0.00% are unmapped (taxid 0), and 30.9% carry placeholder (MAG-like) names whose lineage stops at phylum/class.
- **Tiers are different populations:** tier 1 = 46 complete reference proteomes (model organisms and pathogens, eukaryote-heavy), tier 2 = Swiss-Prot (curated, well-studied, higher pLDDT), tier 3 = TrEMBL remainder. Tier 1/2 are v6 models from AFDB files; tier 3 are v4 Foldcomp-decompressed models — model version/source is a covariate (POCKETOME §6.3, §6.10); never compare the same protein across sources.
- **Accession dedup across tiers:** each accession appears in its first tier only, so a tier-3 taxon that also has Swiss-Prot entries has those proteins in tier 2 (counted as present in §5).
- **Filters shape content:** pockets with BURROW score < 0.03 or lining pLDDT < 70 are dropped and pLDDT < 50 residues pruned; low-confidence/disordered proteins and clades with lower pLDDT have fewer kept pockets per structure (see quality tables). `frac_pockets_kept` differs by tier and superkingdom.
- **`source` column vs the guide:** tier-1 structure tables store the proteome tar name (`UP…_v6.tar`) in `source`, not `afdb_v6_tar` as POCKETOME §3 says; tier-1 pocket tables lack the column. Normalise by tier, never by string equality on `source`.
- **Empty units:** units with 0 kept pockets write a pocket file without a `source` column even in tier 3; read with `union_by_name=true` and fill `source` from the tier.
- **Fragments:** long human proteins are split into overlapping fragments in tier 1 (structures > accessions); deduplicate by residue overlap before per-protein counts.
- **Strain-level taxids:** many tier-3 taxids are strains/isolates of the same species; species-level counts collapse them by NCBI species name.
